Privacy

Your files stay with you. SHIP Server runs on your own computer or cloud machine. Files, user accounts and transfer history are stored only there. We do not receive, store or have access to them.

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking. When you use the contact form, your email program opens with your message; we only receive what you choose to send.

The SHIP apps connect only to the SHIP Server address you enter. SHIP Server may contact public services to find its public IP address and to set up router port forwarding; you can turn automatic port mapping off in Settings.
